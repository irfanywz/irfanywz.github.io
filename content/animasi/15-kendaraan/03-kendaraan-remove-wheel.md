### RECONSTRUCTION & REPAIR
After removing the wheels:
* Naturally reconstruct and fill in the areas that were hidden behind the wheels (such as wheel arches, mudguards, lower chassis, suspension housings, or underbody sections).
* Match the reconstructed areas seamlessly with the surrounding vehicle body, colors, linework, outlines, cel shading, and overall materials.

### PRESERVE 100%
Do NOT change, redesign, move, or modify any other part of the vehicle, including:
* overall vehicle identity and silhouette
* main body, cabin, windows, doors, and roof
* bumpers, lights, mirrors, and grille
* proportions, perspective, and camera angle
* art style and outline thickness

### RESTRICTIONS
* Do NOT replace the removed wheels with other objects.
* Do NOT add new elements, text, labels, or extra details.
* Maintain a clean background (solid white or transparent style).
