            this.simplifyDesc = rand.description;
            this.activeImage = rand.image;

* Focus **ONLY on the minimal shape language and texture reduction level**
* Note how much detail is stripped from complex surfaces
* **DO NOT describe the specific large physical scene elements**
* Keep the text short, clean, and directly usable for the simplify tool

**Output ONE detail description sentence only.**`;

- major objects
