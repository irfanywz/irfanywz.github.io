Use the attached **CHARACTER IMAGE** as the **STRICT CHARACTER REFERENCE**.

Use the attached **OUTFIT IMAGE** as the **STRICT OUTFIT REFERENCE**.

Create the **EXACT SAME CHARACTER** wearing the outfit shown in the OUTFIT IMAGE.

### OUTFIT TRANSFER

Apply the clothing design from the OUTFIT IMAGE **as accurately as possible** to the character.

Follow the reference for:

* garment type and layers
* upper and lower clothing
* silhouette, cut, and length
* sleeves and collar
* colors and patterns
* materials and visible texture
* pockets, buttons, seams, and distinctive details
* footwear
* other details that are clearly part of the outfit

**Do NOT redesign the outfit.** Transfer the actual clothing design from the OUTFIT IMAGE, then naturally adapt it to the character's existing body proportions and pose.

[NOTE]

### CHARACTER LOCK

Keep the CHARACTER IMAGE completely unchanged:

* identity and age
* head and face
* facial features
* face shape
* hairstyle, hair shape, and hair color
* skin tone
* body shape and proportions
* hands and legs
* pose and body position
* camera angle and perspective
* composition
* original art style

Do NOT copy any person/model from the OUTFIT IMAGE.

### OUTFIT IMAGE LOCK

From the OUTFIT IMAGE, transfer **ONLY the outfit**.

Do NOT transfer:

* face
* head
* hairstyle
* hair color
* skin tone
* body shape
* body proportions
* pose
* identity
* unrelated accessories

If an accessory is clearly part of the outfit, include it; otherwise, do not add it.

### VISUAL CONSISTENCY

Render the transferred outfit using the CHARACTER IMAGE's existing visual style:

* same linework
* same outline quality
* same color treatment
* same proportions
* same shading
* same rendering
* same level of detail
* simple, clean, animation-friendly 2D design

The outfit must look naturally designed for the original character.

### DO NOT ALTER

Do NOT change the character's:

* head or face
* hairstyle or hair color
* skin tone
* body or proportions
* hands or legs
* pose or body position
* camera angle
* perspective
* composition
* identity

Do NOT add props, extra characters, text, backgrounds, or unrelated accessories.

### REFERENCE PRIORITY

**CHARACTER IMAGE → identity, body, pose, composition, visual style.**
**OUTFIT IMAGE → clothing, footwear, and outfit-specific details.**

Do not mix the two references.

### FINAL RESULT

Same character + same pose + same body + **ONLY the outfit transferred from the OUTFIT IMAGE**.

**ONLY CHANGE THE OUTFIT.**
